TauGuard

What TauGuard builds

One company, several systems that are not one product. Everything on this page is TauGuard's own description, read on 24 Nov 2026, with the page it came from.

One company, multiple architectures

TauGuard says it is "the company", and that its systems "are not interchangeable implementations of one product": "ARCHONT GOS and TauDIL GOS are separate products. TauDIL GOS is not a renamed ARCHONT GOS component, and ARCHONT GOS is not a replacement for TauDIL GOS."

Source: tauguard.ai/systems.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

ARCHONT GOS

A Governance Operating System for organisational state and change. TauGuard says it maintains "the governed relationship between purpose, authority, meaning, canonical knowledge, rules, evidence, dependencies, assessments, current state and operational consequence". It includes TauMesh and TauStamp.

Source: tauguard.ai/archon.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

TauDIL GOS

A separate Governance Operating System for intelligent systems and intelligence-producing processes: deterministic intelligence governance, semantic coherence and semantic drift, "with SYGON integrated into the architecture".

Source: tauguard.ai/taudil.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

SYGON

Described as a semantic governance layer that does not generate the answer. TauGuard's line: "When AI does not know, it should know not to invent." Its four steps: ground, context, evaluate, decide (allow or refuse).

Source: tauguard.ai/sygon.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

ROD

Gamified PC security "designed to make protective behaviour visible, understandable and engaging". TauGuard says: "The system is under development." We say no more than that.

Source: tauguard.ai/rod.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

IEL Guard

TauGuard's page says IEL Guard "enforces AI behavioral constraints with cryptographic proof", in three steps: define invariants, enforce on every action, anchor proof on-chain. The page states "100% action coverage" and "under 1ms enforcement latency" as figures; we have not tested either. TauGuard's own systems overview lists three systems and links IEL from its menu, so we show it as a fourth item and say so.

Source: tauguard.ai/iel.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

Frameworks, shown apart

TauGuard says the frameworks "are distinct from TauGuard products. A framework establishes a research or architectural model; a system implements capabilities for a particular governed environment." It lists five: IFA (Intelligence From Architecture), SFA (Security From Architecture), GFA (Governance From Architecture), AGL (Authority Governed Learning) and ALA (Admissible Learning Architecture).

Source: tauguard.ai/frameworks.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

Its governance page also names GOM (Governance of Meaning), GUD (Governance Under Degradation) and TauMesh (topological governance) as "governance extensions".

Source: tauguard.ai/governance.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

IFA is published as "Core Specification v1.0".

Source: tauguard.ai/ifa.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

What we have not said

We name nobody at TauGuard. We use no TauGuard logo or branding. Where TauGuard states a number, we repeat it as its claim, not as something we checked.
