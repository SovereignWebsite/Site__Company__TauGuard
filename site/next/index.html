What next

TauGuard publishes its own path for working with others. We start from it, not from ours.

TauGuard's path

TauGuard's partners page sets out "From introduction to deployment" in five steps and says its objective is "to convert credible technical mechanisms into independently evaluated, commercially relevant deployments while keeping claims aligned with the evidence available."

Source: tauguard.ai/partners.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

The page names six kinds of contact, though it introduces them as "five ways": investors, sales channels, independent sales professionals, pilot deployment companies, strategic partners and enterprise evaluators.

Where we could fit

Proposal We are not investors or sellers. The nearest fits are the enterprise evaluator and the strategic partner, and then, if TauGuard wished, a small pilot.

TauGuard's stepWhere we could fit (a proposal)
IntroduceThis site is a way in: it shows we have read the pages and what we would like to test. The Sovereign Website team reviews it before TauGuard sees it.
EvaluateThe architecture drawings on this site are an outside reading of its design. We would like TauGuard to correct them.
PilotThe MVP is smaller than a pilot. It could be a first step toward one.
ValidateThe measures on the MVP page are ours to propose. TauGuard would decide what counts as evidence.
ScaleNot for us to say.

What happens now

Dinis Cruz decides whether and when this site is shown to TauGuard, and what from any meeting goes into it. Until TauGuard agrees, no TauGuard person is named here and no TauGuard branding is used.
