Two companies, one boundary

TauGuard governs what may happen. RiskMandate writes down what each agent may do, and puts something in the way of the gap. Both are about the same place: the line between an agent's intent and its consequence.

TauGuard

Governs what may happen

TauGuard says: "Intelligence may propose. Deterministic governance evaluates admissibility. Execution occurs only when the governing conditions are satisfied."

Source: tauguard.ai/governance.html, read 24 Nov 2026.
RiskMandate

Writes down what each agent may do

RiskMandate says it can "tell you what each one can actually do, write down what you authorised it to do, and put something in the way of the difference."

Source: riskmandate.ai, read 24 Nov 2026.
