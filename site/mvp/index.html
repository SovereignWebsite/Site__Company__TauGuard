MVP

A two-way MVP

One small thing that works in both directions, so each side learns what the other's design does under real use.

Proposal All of this is a proposal. Nothing here is agreed with TauGuard or built. The first thing we need from TauGuard is an endpoint or a demo we can call, and we do not assume it exists.

Ours: a keeper's push to dev

One of our agents, proposed as a keeper pushing to dev, acts only after a TauGuard APPROVE. REVIEW holds it for a person. BLOCK stops it, and the stop is final for that attempt. We chose this because the repository already has a rule that keepers push to dev only and a signing check, so we can compare TauGuard's decision with what the existing checks would have done.

Theirs: one article through TauDIL

One PrompTugal News article has its claims checked for semantic coherence by TauDIL, with SYGON, before the newsroom's own gate. We keep the gate. We see whether TauDIL's check agrees with it, and whether it reports drift.

What we would measure

MeasureMeaning
DecisionsHow many calls or claims were decided, and how each came out.
Correct and false blocksA block is correct if a person, looking later, agrees. A false block is one they overturn.
Added latencyThe time the check added to the push or the article.
PublishedWhat actually went out, and whether anything wrong did.

What we need from TauGuard

  • An endpoint or demo for an outside call. We do not assume one exists. TauGuard's pages offer a downloadable ARCHONT simulator and a TauDIL interactive demo; neither is known to take our input.
  • A statement of what TauGuard will agree to publish. We publish nothing about TauGuard's results without it.
  • Its own view of whether the two measurements are fair to its design.
Source: tauguard.ai/partners.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

Its partners page says pilots are "subject to technical qualification, scope, security requirements, commercial terms, data considerations and a mutually agreed written pilot agreement". This MVP is smaller than a pilot and would not replace one.
