Together

Side by side

Five places where a RiskMandate idea and a TauGuard idea look like the same thing from two sides. The pairing is our proposal. Neither company has said it.

RiskMandate saysTauGuard saysOur reading (a proposal)
The ABP: what each agent may do (reach, mandate, gap, barrier)An executable constitution: rules that run, not documents. TauGuard: "A narrative describes. An executable constitution determines."Different jobs. The ABP describes; the constitution decides. W1 asks whether a mandate can become predicates.
The gap: what an agent can do beyond its mandateAdmissibility: whether an action is authorised under the governed state. If it cannot be shown, no execution.RiskMandate records the gap and, at the summit, allowed it. TauGuard would refuse it. That is the interesting difference.
Hooks (a rule checked on every call) and SHarness (held by something the agent cannot reach)The execution boundary: consequence-bearing action sits behind it.Both place enforcement outside the model. RiskMandate says it is never in the request path; TauGuard's core is active at the boundary. W1 and W4 depend on which.
Run reports: one per agent run, against its ABPChronicle (evidence and lineage committed) and, in ARCHONT, TauStamp (governance memory for legitimacy replay).Both keep a record meant to be checked later by someone else. Whether one can feed the other is untested.
"The rule that failed became a barrier": a sentence, then a breach, then a controlStructural refusal (GOV-04): where admissibility cannot be shown, refusal is terminal.RiskMandate arrived at a barrier after a failure; TauGuard starts from refusal. Same end state, different route.
Sources: Source: tauguard.ai/governance.html, read 24 Nov 2026. Source: riskmandate.ai, read 24 Nov 2026. Source: tauguard.ai/architecture.html, read 24 Nov 2026.

Where they differ

  • Describe or decide. An ABP "describes and does not judge". TauGuard's core returns APPROVE, REVIEW or BLOCK.
  • In the path or beside it. RiskMandate: "We are still never in the request path." TauGuard: governance is "active at the execution boundary rather than reconstructed afterwards".
  • Observed or proven. RiskMandate's summit counts were observed in observe mode and "nobody outside the team has checked" them. TauGuard says its mechanisms can be examined "within their stated scope" and publishes a simulator for ARCHONT.

These differences are why the pairing could be useful. They are also the first things TauGuard may want to correct.
